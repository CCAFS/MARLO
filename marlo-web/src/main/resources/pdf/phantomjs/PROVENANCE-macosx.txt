  https://github.com/Medium/phantomjs/releases/download/v2.1.1/phantomjs-2.1.1-macosx.zip
  (the download source of the phantomjs-prebuilt npm package)

  538cf488219ab27e309eafc629e2bcee9976990fe90b1ec334f541779150f8c1

The binary is a 64-bit x86_64 Mach-O executable (it runs on Apple Silicon through Rosetta 2).

(and in the Linux tarball and the Windows zip). The licences and the source of the
statically linked LGPL components are in LGPL-2.1.txt and SOURCE.txt.
